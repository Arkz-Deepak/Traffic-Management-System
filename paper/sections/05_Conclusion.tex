\section{Conclusion and Future Work}
\label{sec:conclusion}

This paper presented \textbf{AURA}, a decentralized Deep Reinforcement Learning framework with Deterministic Safety Envelopes designed for adaptive traffic signal optimization and resilient emergency vehicle preemption. By bounding a 64-neuron Deep Q-Network within hard physical limits ($10\,\text{s} \le t_{\text{green}} \le 45\,\text{s}$) and enforcing mandatory 3-second yellow clearance intervals, AURA eliminates phase flickering and starvation hazards endemic to unconstrained reinforcement learning controllers.

Rigorous microscopic simulation within Eclipse SUMO under the Krauss car-following model demonstrated that AURA delivers steady-state civilian queue efficiency ($\bar{Q} = 0.85$ vehicles) competitive with theoretical Max-Pressure ($\bar{Q} = 0.76$) and demand-responsive actuated controllers ($\bar{Q} = 0.81$), while drastically outperforming traditional fixed-time schemes ($\bar{Q} = 2.02$). Crucially, under emergency preemption, AURA cuts EV intersection transit latency by 58.6\% (14\,s vs. 34\,s and 30\,s) and absorbs the resulting cross-street congestion shockwave within a recovery horizon of $T_{\text{rec}} = 20$\,s. 

Furthermore, empirical scaling to an authentic 183-intersection OpenStreetMap network of Midtown Manhattan confirmed the real-world viability of our approach: AURA reduced average arterial queues by 31.0\% (26.33 vs. 38.19 vehicles) and curtailed EV clearance latency by 61.9\% (16\,s vs. 42\,s) relative to fixed-time municipal plans, demonstrating that the deterministic safety envelope successfully insulates learning agents from state-space explosion on large metropolitan grids.

Future work will focus on physical hardware-in-the-loop (HIL) deployment across NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano edge-compute modules. We intend to integrate real-time acoustic siren classification models with ROS~2 Jazzy and Data Distribution Service (DDS) middleware to establish zero-SPOF, peer-to-peer neighborhood state sharing across distributed municipal intersections.
